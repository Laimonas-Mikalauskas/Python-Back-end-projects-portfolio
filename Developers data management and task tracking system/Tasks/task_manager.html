Welcome to the first task management app!!!

This is a simple task management app built with Flask

It allows users to create, view, update and delete tasks.

Features:

  • Create new tasks with a title and description.
  • View a list of all tasks.
  • View details of individual tasks.
  • Edit existing tasks to update their information.
  • Update existing tasks.
  • Delete tasks that are no longer needed.
  • User-friendly interface for easy task management.
  • Built with Flask, a lightweight web framework for Python.
  • Perfect for students, professionals, and anyone looking to stay organized."
  • Helps boost productivity and keep track of important tasks.
  • Free and open-source, allowing for customization and improvement.

Get Started:

To get started, simply click on the 'Create Task' button and fill out the form with your task details. You can then view your tasks on the homepage, where you can also edit or delete them as needed. This app is perfect for anyone looking to stay organized and manage their tasks efficiently. Whether you're a student, professional, or just someone who wants to keep track of their to-do list, this task management app has got you covered! Try it out and see how it can help you stay on top of your tasks and boost your productivity! Happy task managing!
